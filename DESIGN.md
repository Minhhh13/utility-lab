## Surface roles

## System

- Self-hosted **Manrope** provides the type system. Use clear weight/size steps, readable metadata and phone text inputs of at least 16px.
- `utility-lab/src/theme.css` owns semantic surface, canvas, text, border, accent and marker tokens. Light/Dark/System and the Cobalt/Ember/Mint/Rose palettes update the whole workspace, including map treatment and inspector.
- Light mode uses warm off-white chrome and a cool map field; dark mode uses graphite surfaces. Accent indicates selection and primary actions. Team and utility colors also have labels or recognizable shapes.
- Ordinary controls use one SVG icon family. Smoke, flash, molly and HE share recognizable grenade silhouettes through `UtilityIcon.tsx` across tools, markers, lists and statistics.
- `BrandMark.tsx` draws the original route symbol with three circular nodes and a diamond endpoint. The displayed glyph is complete and has no square border or background. Its ink follows the accent palette and theme, without a bevel or looping shine. Favicon, web installation icons and prepared native assets share the canonical geometry; installation icons retain a flat platform icon field.
- Borders separate adjacent working areas. Shadows identify floating controls and overlays; they do not decorate every panel.
- The global header has no Local workspace connectivity label or green status dot. Workspace storage and backup behavior is explained in the settings and save flows.

## Content and reference use

Real map geometry and genuine lineup media are working content. Preserve linked **[Minimaps by setpos.gg](https://setpos.gg)** credit for its vector artwork, and keep creator attribution and personal-media provenance separate from application branding. Approximate pins and source-linked examples are not represented as independently verified throws.

Remote photo URLs reference unchanged HTTPS assets on their original source servers and remain references in backups, separate from uploaded photo bytes and the bundled offline photo pack. The browser may retain up to twelve successfully decoded publisher photos for seven days. Confirmation and retry are isolated by browser tab before an image is persisted; failures never block normal image display. Warm only one adjacent slide after the current image loads, at low priority and with data-saving preferences respected. No video cache, bulk background photo download, proxy or database is introduced. Historical full-source-page iframe audits remain research evidence; they do not describe the current photo-viewer design.
