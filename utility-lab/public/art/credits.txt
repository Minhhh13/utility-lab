Utility Lab | Counter-Strike 2 map backgrounds

Source imagery © Valve Corporation and the respective map creators.
Utility Lab applies the pixel treatment to Valve-distributed Panorama views of the actual maps. The original views are 1920 × 1080. Each 640 × 360 background uses a map-specific colour grade, edge accents and a 56-colour palette. The picker previews are 96 × 54.

Utility Lab is an unofficial, noncommercial fan project.
Valve's fan-art terms are in the Steam Subscriber Agreement, section 2.D.
https://store.steampowered.com/subscriber_agreement/#2
