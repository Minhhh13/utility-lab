## What the records establish

- Exact video IDs, creators, publication dates and selected chapter offsets are source metadata from YouTube. The guides are explicitly about **CS2**, including the July 2026 Cache video; no CS:GO Cache tutorial is substituted.
- Origins are named only where the description names them. Cache's CT A-main chapter names the target but not its standing position, so the app directs the player to the video for that position.
- T/CT grouping follows the named tactical setup. It is a navigation category, not a claim that a throw can only be used by that side.
- Map coordinates are approximate interface markers. They are not source-measured landing points, grenade volumes, trajectories, stand positions or exact aim coordinates. They must not be marketed as verified coverage.
- The app's steps describe how to learn from a video. Precise movement, jump, mouse-button and release inputs remain in the original demonstration. No unobserved left-click, right-click, combined-button or jump-throw instruction is asserted.

## Remaining gaps

- These 16 examples cover one smoke per side on each supported map. They do not provide a complete library of smokes, flashes, molotovs and HE grenades.
- None has been independently retested in the installed November 2026 game build. In particular, Nuke and Vertigo use 2024 guides, and Anubis was published in January 2026. A year in an editable title does not establish compatibility with later geometry changes.
- Aim-photo slides require original captures or permission from their owner. There are no fabricated aim screenshots in this data.
- Before describing any lineup as tested, record the game build, map version, original capture, standing position, aim, mouse buttons, movement and successful repeat throws in practice.
