# Home map artwork

Home offers eight pixel-art treatments of the map views Valve ships in Counter-Strike 2. Each starts with a 1920 × 1080 Panorama screenshot. A map-specific grade, edge accents and a 56-colour palette give the landmarks a consistent pixel treatment. Each 640 × 360 scene is a lossless WebP. The picker uses separate 96 × 54 previews. Dust II opens by default. Home remembers the selected map on this device. Opening the picker loads the previews, and selecting a map loads its full scene. Portrait layouts keep the map’s landmark in view. An explicit offline map download includes the eight scenes, the picker previews and credits.
