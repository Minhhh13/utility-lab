# Home artwork

All six scenes use published pixel art by Luis Zuno (ansimuz). The selected scene links to the artist's source from Home. The distributed credits identify the artist, license and compositing or cropping for each file. The two works licensed CC BY 3.0 in the artist's downloads are Cyberpunk Street Environment and Magic Cliffs Environment. For Magic Cliffs, the source page also offers CC0. The app credits that work under the license included in the downloaded archive. The remaining four artist notices dedicate the art to the public domain. Only the original artwork files were used. [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) · [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The canvas fills widescreen, 4:3 and portrait viewports by cropping around a scene-specific focal point. Native pixels scale to integer physical display pixels, including at 3840 × 2160. The artwork keeps the level of detail in its source. Lossless WebP stores the seven images in 54,562 bytes total. A normal visit requests only the chosen scene.
