# Home motion

Map changes crossfade for 300 ms. The previous picture stays visible while the selected map decodes. When the new frame extends past the viewport, it moves up to three original pixels in three steps over 540 ms. At an exact fit the map stays in place. The crop keeps the chosen landmark visible on a phone.

The scene entrance runs once. Sparse dust drifts across original Dust, three ripples follow the water on Aztec, and snow falls in front of the Office façades. These details stay anchored to the illustration when it crops on a phone. Hidden tabs pause the animation. Reduced motion shows the still painting and workspace controls immediately. Returning by keyboard restores focus to the last workspace. [Classic map paintings](launch-art.md)
