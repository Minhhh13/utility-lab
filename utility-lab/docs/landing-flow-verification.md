# Map-only landing flow verification

The current path exposes only necessary choices on the map. After active filters, one available utility and one exact destination go directly from a group to standing pins; one utility with several destinations shows landing pins directly. A multi-utility group opens a radial; choosing a type shows landing pins only for multiple exact destinations, otherwise it shows standing pins. Photos require a standing-pin selection, even for a single start. There is no ordinary right sidebar, named-location list or landing-choice list. The limit of four standing recipes is applied only after the destination is resolved.

The public website is [utilitylab.au](https://utilitylab.au), with local development at http://127.0.0.1:5180. Final executed results and production/package evidence are recorded in [verification.md](verification.md). Earlier chooser-based logs describe a superseded interface. They do not establish that the current map-only path passed a later production run.

## Contracts to verify

- Fit retains every semantic area represented by the complete matching catalogue. Nearby main areas share a neutral 36px marker with real utility glyphs and a 46px hit area. Its centre is an actual member coordinate; there is no overview badge displacement or leader grid. Pan moves markers with the map. Zoom refines groups while preserving their membership. All eight maps, ten floor views and both sides are covered; an empty floor remains an honest empty state with recovery rather than fabricated content.
- A single available utility skips the radial, whether it comes from a global filter or the group itself. A single exact destination skips the landing step and exposes real standing pins. Multiple exact destinations remain selectable as map pins; point identity includes semantic area, map, destination floor, side, utility type and the two-decimal published coordinate. The source-choice cap cannot remove a landing. Multiple recipes at one point stay together. A single standing recipe still waits for an explicit pin selection before opening photos.
- Cache T Mid Smoke exposes its distinct Left Mid and Right Mid landings. A nonfirst landing must lead only to recipes for that exact point and open the corresponding original Position photo URL. Dust II Mid Doors remains available alongside nearby Lower Tunnels and Xbox. Nuke Secret Smoke retains all of its registered points rather than only the coarser zoom clusters.
- Group, utility, landing and standing selections, opening/closing photos, and Back to targets preserve the current map viewBox on the same floor, both at Fit and after manual zoom/pan. Pin focus must not scroll the map. Manual wheel/pinch zoom still reaches 4×, with pan and Fit available. Changing to a different map or floor retains normal fitting behavior. Source coordinates are not relocated.
- Keyboard focus advances to the next necessary choice: utility, landing or standing pins. Enter/Space selects map hotspots. The utility radial retains dismissal and keyboard navigation. Keyboard and reduced-motion entry remain immediate. A single Saved guide with one destination goes straight to standing pins; a Smoke-only group skips the radial and skips the landing step only if its exact destination is unique. The full path must fit 320px without a hidden reference list.
- While the utility radial is open, all overview markers are hidden. Utility choices must remain clear of the removed static chooser hint; closing the radial restores the overview layer.
- Delayed standing-location JSON waits before exposing source pins, including when a single destination skips the landing step. Returning to targets or dismissing the pending choice cancels the request so stale work cannot reopen a selection. Source coordinates, source-supported floors, cross-floor transitions and source-photo bindings remain separate from destination metadata.
- Viewer Save/Unsave updates favorites. Its ellipsis menu preserves Copy lineup link, Download lineup, Make my own and personal Edit/Delete actions. Personal editing is explicit, saving reopens the viewer, and portable exports/backups preserve uploaded photos. Normal browsing stays map-only.
- Display settings must group In-game view, persistent Auto / 16:9 / 4:3 desktop frames and Hide top bar. The floating Show top bar control remains reachable with a 44px hit area. Narrow phones auto-fit while retaining the desktop frame preference. Changing display/frame/header state must preserve map geometry and editor records. Home stays full-screen; the saved ratio applies to the working tabs.
- Playbooks remains greyed out as Coming soon. Direct routes show a placeholder while existing plan data and backup models remain intact. The restored route symbol remains complete and unboxed; decorative motion belongs to the classic Home scenes, not the operational map.

Executable coverage lives in `tests/utility-markers.test.ts`, `tests/board-navigation.test.ts` and the relevant `e2e/` overview, landing, origin, navigation, viewer and backup suites. UI-only fixtures can substitute small image bytes while retaining original source URLs and registered coordinates; those checks prove state/provenance bindings, not fresh decoding or playback of every online resource. Original-media evidence remains in the separate source and media audits.
