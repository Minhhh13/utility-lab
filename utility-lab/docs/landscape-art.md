# Launch landscape artwork

Original decorative art for Utility Lab's launch screen. These images are not game screenshots, map geometry or lineup evidence. The existing courtyard remains the default. Three new scenes were generated with the built-in image generation tool on 9 November 2026; no CLI/API fallback was used. Original outputs remain in the tool's generated-images directory; selected final WebPs are copied into `public/art/`.

The app requests only the selected landscape. Each final asset is below 1 MB; conversion changes the encoding without changing the artwork's dimensions. `launch-pixel.png` remains the original courtyard source.

## Dunes

Final asset: `public/art/launch-dunes.webp`.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: fullscreen landscape background for Utility Lab, a Counter-Strike tactical planning app. Create one ORIGINAL wide 16:9 pixel-art environment, no UI. Scene: an empty sun-baked desert fortress on a sandstone canyon ridge, arched sandstone passages and a few stacked wooden supply crates in the middle distance, quiet sandy path and weathered stone parapet in the foreground, far dunes and soft mesa silhouettes. Late golden-hour peach sky, warm amber highlights and cool blue-violet shadows. High-craft hand-authored 16-bit adventure-game pixel art, crisp deliberate square pixel clusters, rich architectural depth and restrained detail, visible pixels rather than soft painted texture. Composition: panoramic landscape with clear focal architecture around center-right and open calm sky/negative space in upper left; environment remains interesting when center-cropped on phone. No people, weapons, explosions, HUD, logos, labels, text or watermark. No copied videogame map geometry or screenshots. Fill entire image, no borders or letterboxing. Match a cohesive sophisticated retro pixel environment style, not photorealistic and not vector.
