# Apple HIG notes for the tactic board

[Dick Wu's skill](https://github.com/dickwu/apple-design-skill) was read together with relevant foundation and interaction references: accessibility, layout, typography, color, designing for iOS, cross-platform translation, buttons, undo and redo, pointing devices, and toolbars.

The current artifact is a React web app. Use Apple's foundations as design principles, retaining web semantics and familiar browser controls. Native toolbar, menu-bar and tab-bar rules are not claims about this draft.

- [Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility): support text enlargement and alternative inputs. Pair color with text or shape. Keep important actions reachable without complex gestures.
- [Layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout): order content by importance, group related actions, align shared edges and reveal secondary complexity progressively.
- [Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography): preserve hierarchy as type grows, use legible weights and allow text to wrap rather than disappear.
- [Color](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color): assign colors consistent meanings and test their actual surroundings; do not depend on hue alone.
- [Undo and redo](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/undo-and-redo): make recovery predictable, preserve multiple steps and visibly show the result.
- [Toolbars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars): prioritize frequent commands, separate navigation from editing and keep groups small.

For this board, craft means a map that leads, familiar editing controls, immediate manipulation and reliable recovery. Glass effects and decorative motion do not help the match-time task.
